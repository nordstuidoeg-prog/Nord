About Maison

Crafted with intention,
built to last.

Founded in 2008 by designer Isabelle Fontaine, Maison has spent over a decade defining what luxury interior design means in the modern era. We believe that truly exceptional spaces are not merely decorated — they are composed.

Our studio operates from Paris and New York, serving private clients, boutique hotels, and flagship retail brands who demand nothing less than the extraordinary. From concept sketches to final styling, every decision is purposeful.

We do not mass-produce. We do not compromise. We listen, we design, we execute — and the result is a space that will be admired for generations.

What we offer

Every client engagement begins with deep listening. We immerse ourselves in your world, your habits, your aspirations — before a single concept is drawn. Our services cover the full spectrum of interior transformation.

01
Residential Design

Complete home transformations from a single statement room to entire residences. We craft living spaces that reflect your identity while elevating daily life to a new standard.

02
Commercial Interiors

Offices, boutiques, restaurants, and hotels designed to command attention and project brand authority. We translate business values into physical environments that perform.

03
Art Consulting

Curated art selection from our network of galleries and emerging artists. We place works that converse with your space, building collections that appreciate in value and meaning.

04
Bespoke Furniture

Collaboration with master craftsmen in France and Italy to produce one-of-a-kind furniture pieces. Designed in our atelier, built by hand, finished to perfection.

05
Lighting Design

Light is the invisible material. Our lighting concepts sculpt mood, define architecture, and bring each room to life at every hour of the day — from dawn golden light to late evening intimacy.

06
Project Management

Full oversight from contractor selection to installation day. Our team ensures that your project is delivered on time, within budget, and exactly as envisioned — with zero compromise.

The People Behind the Spaces

Meet the team

Isabelle Fontaine
Founder & Creative Director

Trained at the École Boulle in Paris and the Royal College of Art in London, Isabelle founded Maison in 2008 with a singular obsession: spaces that feel inevitable. Her work has been featured in Architectural Digest and Elle Décor.

Rafael Alves
Principal Designer

With a decade of experience in luxury hotel interiors across Europe and Asia, Rafael brings a global sensibility to every Maison project. His ability to balance opulence with restraint defines the studio's signature aesthetic.

Chloé Laurent
Head of Art Consulting

A former curator at the Musée d'Art Moderne in Paris, Chloé leads Maison's art acquisition and placement division. She maintains deep relationships with galleries in Paris, New York, and Tokyo to bring rare pieces to our clients.
