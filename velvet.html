The
Velvet
Ritual

Skincare is most powerful as a practice — not a panic. The Velvet Ritual is a four-step morning and evening sequence designed around clinical evidence and sensory pleasure. Each product performs at its peak when used in sequence.

We believe that good skin isn't about buying more. It's about using less, better. Our formulations are intentionally concentrated so a little goes a long way — for your skin, and for the planet.

01 💧
Cleanse

Begin with our Micellar Cleansing Water or Cream Cleanser. Removes makeup, SPF, and daily buildup without stripping the skin barrier.

02 ✨
Treat

Apply your serum of choice — Radiance No. 3 for brightening, Retinal No. 7 for renewal, or Hydra Complex for intense moisture.

03 🌿
Nourish

Seal in your treatment with Velvet Barrier Cream. Locks in actives, replenishes ceramides, and creates the signature velvet-skin finish.

04 ☀️
Protect (AM)

Complete your morning ritual with SPF Mist 50+. Invisible, non-sticky, and reef-safe. The most important step you're probably skipping.

Clean Formulation

Rare ingredients.
Honest science.

Every Velvet ingredient is selected against a strict five-point criteria: clinically evidenced efficacy, sustainability of source, skin tolerance, environmental impact, and sensory experience. We refuse to compromise on any of them.

Our formulation team reviews the full INCI list of every product annually to ensure we stay ahead of emerging research — and we publish the concentration of every active ingredient on each product page.

Our Story

Born from a
need for truth
in beauty.

Velvet was founded in 2018 by Camille Dubois, a former cosmetic chemist who spent a decade formulating for luxury beauty conglomerates before deciding she could do better — with full transparency and a genuine commitment to skin science.

What started as a three-product line sold at a London farmers market is now a full luxury skincare brand carried in 24 countries. Every product is still formulated by Camille's team in our London lab, and every batch is QC-tested before it leaves the building.
