We go
out there
too.

Nomad Gear was started in 2012 by three mountaineers who were fed up with gear that looked good in catalogues and failed on the mountain. We design in the field, test at altitude, and refuse to ship anything we wouldn't trust with our own lives.

Every product in our line has been carried up peaks, dragged through rivers, and beaten into submission at test camps in the Alps, Patagonia, and the Scottish Highlands. If it makes the cut, it makes the store.
