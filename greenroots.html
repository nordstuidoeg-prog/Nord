Why GreenRoots?

Not all organic
is equal

  • 🌿
    Certified Soil Association Organic
    Every farm in our network holds full Soil Association certification. We audit annually — because your trust is everything.
  • 📦
    100% Plastic-Free Packaging
    Compostable trays, recycled cardboard, and natural wool insulation. Our boxes biodegrade within 26 weeks.
  • 🤝
    Fair Pay for Farmers
    We pay above-market rates to our partner farms. 15p from every box goes directly into the GreenRoots Farm Fund.
  • ❄️
    Carbon-Neutral Deliveries
    Our delivery fleet is 100% electric within urban areas. All rural routes are offset through certified reforestation projects.
