// About Me

I make brands
feel necessary.

I'm Omar Hassan — a graphic designer from Cairo, now based between London and remote. I've spent 9 years working across brand identity, editorial design, packaging, and motion — for independent studios, ad agencies, and directly with founders who need design that actually does something.

I believe design should be precise, a little confrontational, and completely unmissable. I don't do safe. I do work that people remember 10 years later.

Currently working independently after stints at Pentagram London and Wieden+Kennedy Amsterdam.
